用展開法求出下列遞迴關係式的**精確**解。

(a) 河內塔：<M t="T(1) = 1" />，<M t="T(n) = 2T(n-1) + 1" />

(b) <M t="T(1) = 1" />，<M t="T(n) = T(n-1) + n" />

<Solution>

**(a) <M t="T(n) = 2^n - 1" />。**

<M d t="\begin{aligned} T(n) &= 2T(n-1) + 1 \\ &= 2[2T(n-2) + 1] + 1 = 4T(n-2) + 2 + 1 \\ &= 8T(n-3) + 4 + 2 + 1 \\ &= \cdots \\ &= 2^{n-1} T(1) + 2^{n-2} + \cdots + 2 + 1 \\ &= 2^{n-1} + (2^{n-1} - 1) = 2^n - 1 \end{aligned}" />

驗算：<M t="T(2) = 3" />、<M t="T(3) = 7" />，與 <M t="2^n - 1" /> 相符。

**(b) <M t="T(n) = \dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}" />。**

<M d t="T(n) = n + (n-1) + \cdots + 2 + T(1) = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} = \Theta(n^2)" />

這一題不是「除以 <M t="b" />」的形式，不能用主定理。

</Solution>
