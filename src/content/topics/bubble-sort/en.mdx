## What bubble sort does

Bubble sort repeatedly compares **adjacent** numbers and swaps them if the left one is larger.

1. Start at the left and compare each adjacent pair, swapping when out of order.
2. After one pass the largest remaining number has been carried to the right end, and that position is fixed.
3. The next pass only needs to cover the part that is not yet fixed.
4. If a pass makes no swaps at all, the array is sorted and we can stop early.

## Why it is O(n²)

n numbers need at most n − 1 passes of at most n − 1 comparisons each: n(n − 1) / 2 comparisons in total. On a reversed array every comparison swaps, so swaps are also n(n − 1) / 2 — the numbers shown by the “Worst case” button above.

On sorted data the first pass makes no swaps and ends after n − 1 comparisons.
