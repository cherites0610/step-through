# Surface brief: home page (/zh-TW/, /en/)

Scope: the site root per locale. Mode: Persuade (landing), code-led build inside the established Plate Section world; no new identity, DESIGN.md unchanged.

Audience/job: Taiwanese CS/EE students, self-learners, interview preppers; teachers second. Action: browse the topic catalogue and open a topic (primary), or jump straight to the simplest one (insertion sort). Proof: a real frame from the insertion-sort trace, rendered statically (zero JS). Constraints: static HTML first viewport, no client JS on this page, zh-TW + en, Taiwanese terminology, no card grids, no eyebrow above headings, drawn SVG marks not glyphs.

Structure chosen by the user: COURSE ROADMAP. Topics are laid out by data-structures course order (sorting, trees, hashing and systems, graphs and DP) as hairline columns; each topic is a row marked live or coming soon.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is the contents plate of the annual: a roadmap you can read in one glance, not a marketing page. Refuses hero-plus-feature-cards and the dense grey course-list look.
OWN-WORLD: Ground #F5F4F6 with edge drift to #FAF6F5/#EEF3EF, ink #16181A, hairline #C9C7C4, periwinkle #DCDDF3 only for live rows on hover and the active specimen cells. Noto Sans TC 400/500 for display and prose; JetBrains Mono for plate numbers, step counters, and data. Registration crosshairs and dot matrix frame the hero specimen only. Live topic = filled square mark; coming soon = dashed square mark plus the words, never colour alone.
STORY: In one viewport the visitor reads that this explains algorithms step by step, sees one real step with its one-sentence reason, and sees which topics exist now and which are coming.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 56px top bar (wordmark, projection toggle, language switch). Left: 44-64px display line, one lead sentence, primary action Browse topics, secondary Start with insertion sort. Right: the registered specimen plate showing insertion sort step 7 of 26 with its explanation sentence. Roadmap begins right under the fold line.
FORM: course-contents plate; signature move is the specimen plate (crosshair corners, dot matrix, active cell inverted to filled ink) built from a real trace frame.
